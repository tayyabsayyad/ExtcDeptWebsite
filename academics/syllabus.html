First Year Exit Option — UG Certificate in Electronics & Telecommunication

Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering | Scheme: DB25-V1 | NSQF Level 4.5

Exit Guidelines PDF (p. 190)

Students exiting after securing a minimum of 42 credits across Semesters I & II are awarded a UG Certificate in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering upon completing an additional 8 credits (4-credit Vocational course + 4-credit Internship/Apprenticeship).

Course Code Vertical Course Name Contact Hours Credits Assessment Scheme
25ET2EX01 Vocational Electronic System Assembly & Maintenance 60 (Practical/Lab) 4 IA: 50 | End Sem (Practical/Viva): 50 | Total: 100
25ET2EX02 Internship Industrial Internship / Apprenticeship 120 (On-site/Online, 2–3 weeks) 4 IA: 50 | End Sem (Viva): 50 | Total: 100
Exit Option Total: 8 Credits Total Marks: 200
Suggested Job Profiles (NSQF Level 4.5)
  • Junior Electronics Technician (Testing & Assembly)
  • Telecommunication Field Associate (Installation & Maintenance)
  • PCB Design & Fabrication Assistant
  • IoT Device Installation Assistant
Core Competencies Developed
  • Electronic component identification (SMD & Through-hole) & ESD safety
  • PCB schematic capture & layout using KiCad/EasyEDA; etching & soldering
  • Hardware assembly, testing & signal tracing using DSOs, multimeters
  • BOM preparation, technical datasheets & IPC-A-610 quality standards

DBIT Autonomous Curriculum Framework (NEP 2020)

Degree Pathways, Credit Allocation & Multiple Entry-Exit Architecture

Undergraduate Degree Pathways
  • B.E. (Major in EXTC) 164 - 169 Credits
  • B.E. with Minor / Honors 182 - 187 Credits
  • B.E. Honors with Research 182 - 187 Credits
Credit Definition & Contact Hours
  • Theory Course: 1 Credit = 15 Contact Hours
  • Laboratory / Workshop: 1 Credit = 30 Contact Hours
  • Internship / Field Work: 1 Credit = 40 Hours / 2 Weeks
  • Community Engagement Project (CEP): 1 Credit = 30 Hours
Multiple Entry & Exit Options
  • Certificate in Engineering (After Year 1):
    42 Credits + 8 Credits (4 Credit Exit Course + 4 Summer Internship)
  • Diploma in Engineering (After Year 2):
    86 / 84 Credits + 8 Credits (4 Credit Exit Course + 4 Summer Internship)
  • B. Vocational Degree (After Year 3):
    125 Credits + 8 Credits (4 Credit Exit Course + 4 Summer Internship)
  • Academic Bank of Credits (ABC): All credits earned are banked in the ABC for lifelong learning flexibility.

Students exiting the First-Year programme after securing a minimum of 42 credits will be awarded a UG Certificate in Electronics and Telecommunication provided they secure an additional 8 credits through the prescribed vocational course and internship/apprenticeship. Internship Assessment Criteria (4 Credits)
  • Industry Mentor Appraisal (20%): Punctuality, safety compliance & troubleshooting.
  • Daily Work Log/Diary (20%): Weekly record of technical tasks performed.
  • Technical Internship Report (30%): Formal documentation of workflows handled.
  • Final Skill Demo & Viva (30%): Hands-on circuit assembly & oral examination.
