About PubTech

PubTech is the flagship research publication compendium created by the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at Don Bosco Institute of Technology (DBIT), Mumbai. It is designed to celebrate and disseminate high-quality technical papers authored by final-year engineering students based on their capstone projects.

Every year, final-year undergraduate students undertake complex, industry-relevant, and society-oriented hardware and software projects. Under the guidance of faculty mentors, these efforts culminate in rigorous research papers, prototypes presented at the annual INNOVEX project exhibition, and publications in prestigious international conferences such as IEEE and refereed journals.
