Industrial Visit • AY 2026-2027

Industrial Visit to CETTM, MTNL, Powai, Mumbai

Centre for Excellence in Telecom Technology and Management (CETTM) | Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering

View / Download Full Report (PDF)
8 September 2026 CETTM, MTNL, Powai, Mumbai 39 BE EXTC Students & 2 Faculty Members Faculty Coordinator: Dr. Aparna M. Telgote, Mr. Deepak Pandav Student Coordinator: Mrunmai Paktekar
Aim & Objectives

To gain practical knowledge of modern telecommunication technologies, networking systems, optical fibre communication, and communication infrastructure through an on-site experiential industrial visit to CETTM, Powai.

  • Understand real-world practical applications of telecommunication and cellular networking technologies.
  • Gain hands-on exposure to modern telecom equipment racks, data transmission lines, and optical distribution frames.
  • Observe and practice optical fibre splicing, cleaving, and handling procedures to minimize signal attenuation.
  • Correlate classroom theoretical concepts in Mobile Communication Systems with telecom industry operations.
Key Technical Demonstrations & Activities
Optical Fibre Splicing

Live demonstration of optical fibre cleaving, core alignment, fusion splicing using precision fusion splicers, and OTDR fault-testing principles.

Telecom & Networking Equipment

Tour of active telecom equipment racks, understanding systematic interconnections, GSM/CDMA network infrastructure, and FTTH broadband systems.

Guest Lecture • AY 2026-2027

Guest Lecture on "Personality Development and Career Guidance"

Organized by Department of EXTC & Training and Placement Cell • Target: Third Year (TE) EXTC

View / Download Report (PDF)
12 October 2026 Resource Person: Ms. Raveena Pitale (Application Support Engineer, L&T Finance Ltd., Alumna 2023) 43 TE EXTC Students Present Faculty Coordinator: Dr. Aparna M. Telgote
Session Overview & Key Takeaways

The Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering organized this interactive guest lecture by distinguished alumna Ms. Raveena Pitale. The session provided TE students with actionable roadmaps for career planning, technical interview preparation, competitive exam strategies, and professional self-development.

Core Topics Delivered:
  • Self-Awareness: Identifying core strengths, embracing constructive feedback, and continuous improvement.
  • Stepping Outside Comfort Zone: Developing adaptability and resilience in facing new technical challenges.
  • LinkedIn Profile Development: Crafting a standout professional profile for internships and job hunting.
  • Technical Skills: Emphasized mastering SQL, Python, and cloud fundamentals tailored to modern industry roles.
  • Higher Education: Preparation frameworks for GRE, IELTS, and technical examinations.
  • Interview Demeanor: Professional body language, confident posture, and meaningful questions for interviewers.

Guest Lecture • AY 2026-2027

Guest Lecture on "Career Opportunities in Networking, Network Security & Mobile Communication"

Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering • Target: Final Year (BE) EXTC

View / Download Report (PDF)
9 October 2026 Resource Person: Mr. Yogesh Hudale (Founder & MD, Telenetwork Technologies Pvt. Ltd.) 40 BE EXTC Students Venue: MECH CR3 Faculty Coordinator: Dr. Aparna M. Telgote
Session Overview & Industry Insights

Delivered by industry expert Mr. Yogesh Hudale, this comprehensive expert talk guided final-year EXTC students through emerging roles and technical requirements in telecom infrastructure, network operations centers (NOC), cloud network administration, and enterprise cybersecurity.

Key Areas Covered:
  • Telecom & 5G Opportunities: Real-world networking challenges, signal routing, and cellular backbone operations.
  • Network Security: Confidentiality, integrity, availability, firewalls, and data protection regulations.
  • Industry Tools: Exposure to modern network packet sniffers, security monitors, and virtualization toolchains.
  • Certifications: Value of industry-recognized credentials (CCNA, CCNP, CEH, AWS/Azure Networking).
  • Professional Responsibility: Cybersecurity ethics, data compliance, teamwork, and project lifecycle management.
  • OBE Attainment: Mapped to PO1, PO2, PO5, PO6, PO8, PO9, PO10, PO11, PO12, PSO1, and PSO2.
