InnoQuest 2025–26: Mini Project Exhibition & Competition

Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering • Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Mumbai (Autonomous)

View Report PDF Download
31 October 2025 9:00 am – 4:00 pm Labs 4, 6 & 7 (1st Floor, C-Wing) & Fr. Mondini Hall 72 Participants • 19 Innovative TE EXTC Teams Faculty Coordinator: Dr. Aparna M. Telgote

Event Overview

The Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (EXTC) at Don Bosco Institute of Technology (DBIT) proudly organized InnoQuest 2025–26 on 31st October 2025.

The event commenced at 9:30 am with an inauguration ceremony by Fr. Mylin Noronha sdb, Assistant Director DBC and Administrator, DBCL, who inspired students to innovate boldly and integrate practical hands-on engineering with theoretical principles.

The project exhibition continued until 12:30 pm across specialized department laboratories (Lab 4, Lab 6, and Lab 7). A total of 72 students from Third Year EXTC presented 19 innovative working projects across cutting-edge domains including IoT, Embedded Systems, Mobile Robotics, Healthcare Monitoring, Automation, and Telecommunication.

A rigorous evaluation was conducted by an esteemed panel of faculty judges alongside final-year (BE) student peer mentors, evaluating teams on criteria mapped to Program Outcomes (POs): problem identification, technical design, real-world relevance, working prototype fidelity, and team presentation. The exhibition concluded with a grand valedictory and prize distribution at 2:30 pm in Fr. Mondini Hall.

Event Objectives
  • Showcase Creativity and Innovation: Provide a platform for students to demonstrate innovative and technical abilities through project-based learning.
  • Encourage Practical Application: Motivate students to apply theoretical concepts to real-world problems and develop fully working prototypes.
  • Inspire Younger Students: Create enthusiasm among FE & SE students by exposing them to advanced project work in the EXTC domain.
Key Event Outcomes
  • 19 Unique Projects Displayed: Addressing social, health, agricultural, and industrial challenges.
  • Collaborative Learning: Fostering multidisciplinary problem-solving and rigorous technical discussions.
  • Motivation for Juniors: Inspiring lower-semester students to engage in project development early.

InnoQuest 2024–25: Mini Project Exhibition & Competition

Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering • Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Kurla West, Mumbai

4 October 2024 9:00 am – 4:00 pm Labs 4 & 6 (1st Floor, C-Wing) & Fr. Mondini Hall 51 Participants • 12 EXTC Teams Faculty Coordinator: Dr. Aparna M. Telgote

Detailed Report & Overview

The Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at Don Bosco Institute of Technology proudly hosted the SE and TE Students' Mini Project Exhibition InnoQuest 2024–25 on October 4, 2024 in C-Wing, 1st Floor EXTC Labs.

The exhibition served as an interactive and competitive platform for students to demonstrate functional engineering models. The exhibition aimed to stimulate curiosity, promote peer learning, and inspire First Year (FE) and ITI students to engage early in problem-solving and apply theoretical concepts from electronics and telecommunications into tangible hardware.

A total of 12 teams from EXTC exhibited projects. The event concluded with an exhilarating prize distribution ceremony held in Fr. Mondini Hall (4th Floor, C-Wing) to honour the dedicated student teams for their innovative spirit.

Key Exhibition Pillars
  • Creativity & Innovation: Providing an experiential avenue to exhibit creative and technical capabilities.
  • Practical Application: Bridging classroom theory to real-world embedded, biomedical, and communication challenges.
  • Junior Engagement: Inspiring FE and ITI scholars to undertake multidisciplinary electronics ventures.

Academic Year 2023–24 • Inaugural TE Edition

“InnoQuest 23”: Mini Project Exhibition Report – 2023-24

Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering • Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Kurla West, Mumbai

View Report PDF Download
3 November 2023 C Wing, 1st Floor EXTC Labs & Fr. Mondini Hall 14 Teams • Subject: Miniproject 2A (TE EXTC) Faculty: Mr. Jithin Isaac & Ms. Aparna M. Telgote (Co-ordinator)
Vision

“Exhibitions of mini projects are like windows into the future, where innovation meets imagination.” — InnoQuest 23 Motto

Event Overview & Community Impact

The Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (EXTC) at Don Bosco Institute of Technology (DBIT) presented the culmination of creativity and technical prowess at the SE and TE Students' Mini Project Exhibition InnoQuest 2023-24, held on the 3rd of November, 2023 at the C Wing, 1st Floor, EXTC labs.

The Mini Project Exhibition served as a comprehensive platform combining a technical mini-project competition with an interactive exhibition. It provided a dedicated venue for Third Year students to exhibit their semester-long work, foster open-ended discussions, and demonstrate the immense real-world potential of hardware prototypes. The event also actively motivated first-year and ITI students to explore practical engineering applications early.

A total of 14 student teams participated in this event. The exhibition concluded with an inspiring valedictory ceremony in Fr. Mondini Hall (4th Floor, C-Wing) where winning teams received cash prizes and certificates of technical excellence.

Key Outcomes
  • Diverse Array of Projects: From STM32 DSP audio processing and digital frequency meters to LoRa communications and cleaning robotics.
  • Interactive Discussions: Vibrant atmosphere of knowledge exchange between students, faculty mentors, and DBIT management.
  • Junior Inspiration: Ignite curiosity among lower-cohort students into the realm of hardware design.

Academic Evaluation Rubrics

Standardized Department Assessment Framework Mapped to NBA Program Outcomes (POs)

Criteria Mapped POs Assessment Indicators (Max 10M)
Problem Identification & Planning PO1, PO2, PO3 Real-world/social problem clearly identified with strong planning, feasible timeline, and clear objectives.
Methodology, Analysis & System Design PO3, PO4, PO5 Comprehensive analysis and comparison with existing systems; effective STM32/ESP32 implementation, well-justified components.
Social / Environmental / Industrial Relevance PO6, PO7 Strong, sustainable, and measurable social/environmental/industrial impact clearly demonstrated.
Working Model / Functionality PO3, PO5, PO11 Fully functional prototype demonstrating accuracy, reliability, hardware-software integration, and seamless operation.
Presentation & Teamwork PO9, PO10, PO12 Excellent professional presentation; strong collaboration; demonstrates self-learning and initiative in continuous technologies.
