About Sankalpana

Sankalpana is the flagship project competition of the EXTC Department at DBIT, where students showcase their innovative projects and technical achievements. The event provides a platform for students across all years to demonstrate their skills, present working models, and compete for recognition in a wide range of domains including embedded systems, IoT, communication, signal processing, and more.

Key Project Domains & Tracks

Embedded Systems Internet of Things (IoT) Wireless & RF Communication Signal & Image Processing Robotics & Automation VLSI & Hardware Design
