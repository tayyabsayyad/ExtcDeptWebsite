Tech Canvas 2026 — Live Poster-Making Competition

Theme: "Engineering Ideas for Viksit Bharat" • Organised as part of Independence Day Celebrations

Objectives & Highlights

The IETE-ISF Students' Chapter organized "TechCanvas 2026", a live digital poster-making competition using Canva. Students from diverse engineering disciplines brought forward innovative concepts addressing national societal challenges under the theme "Engineering Ideas for Viksit Bharat".

Design Platform & Format

Live hands-on poster design session where students conceptualized and executed visual engineering solutions in real-time within the allotted time.

Evaluation & Awards

Judged on theme relevance, creativity, technical depth, and visual clarity. Cash prize pool of Rs. 1500 + achievement certificates awarded to top entries.

Industry Expert Interactive Session • 18 August 2026

Applications of AI, GenAI & Agentic AI in Industry

Speaker: Dr. Pushkar M. Sathe (Assistant Manager, Selec Controls Pvt. Ltd., Technical Training - R&D)

View / Download Report (PDF)
18th August 2026 (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM) Mech CR-3, DBIT 54 TE EXTC Students Present Faculty Coordinator: Mrs. Namita Agarwal Documentation Head: Ms. Sakshi Talkar
Key Takeaways & Technical Coverage

Dr. Pushkar M. Sathe delivered an insightful masterclass covering the continuum from traditional machine learning to Generative AI and autonomous Agentic AI. The session emphasized actionable applications in telecom and embedded electronics.

  • Local & Offline LLMs: Utilizing open tools such as Ollama to run and fine-tune private language models on edge hardware.
  • RAG & Embeddings: Retrieval-Augmented Generation architectures for connecting vector databases with enterprise documentation.
  • Autonomous AI Agents: How agents formulate multi-step plans, invoke API tools, and automate workflows with minimal human oversight.
  • EXTC Domain Integrations: Signal processing, smart IoT nodes, predictive maintenance, and embedded system firmware testing.
  • Memento Presentation: Dr. Madhavi Pednekar, HOD EXTC, presented a token of appreciation to Dr. Sathe on behalf of IETE-DBIT-ISF.

Department Celebration • 07 September 2026

Teacher's Day Celebration 2026

Honouring EXTC faculty and staff • Guest of Honour: Principal Dr. Sudhakar Mande

View / Download Report (PDF)
07 September 2026 (3:00 PM – 5:00 PM) EXTC Lab-08, DBIT Teaching & Non-Teaching EXTC Staff & Principal Faculty Coordinator: Mrs. Namita Agarwal Documentation Head: Sakshi Talkar
Event Activities & Fellowship

The IETE-DBIT-ISF student council planned and hosted an affectionate, vibrant celebration for faculty members to unwind from academic duties. Fun-filled intellectual and entertaining challenges were arranged to promote camaraderie and student-faculty bonding.

Electronics Crossword

Tested rapid technical recall of circuit principles in a lighthearted, humorous setting.

Left-Hand Component Sketch

Guessing electronic components hand-sketched with non-dominant hands by faculty teams.

Flowchart Memory Game

Testing concentration and visual memory by reproducing complex algorithm flowcharts.

Musical Chairs & High Tea

Enthusiastic participation followed by distribution of handcrafted mementos and refreshments.
